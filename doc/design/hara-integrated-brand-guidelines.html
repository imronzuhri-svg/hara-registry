The master mark

03
The colour decision

Green root, cool products

The master mark keeps its real HARA green — the living root: agriculture, growth, origin. The products are where data becomes infrastructure, so they wear HARA's product spectrum — a cool teal → blue → indigo gradient for trust, security and exactness — with a warm gold core for the human or valuable thing held inside. Green grows into the cool, exact product layer.

Master · HARA green
A node, warmed → the gold core
Product · cool spectrum + gold

The master logo is never recoloured — it is always HARA green. The cool spectrum and gold core belong to the product marks; together they read as one house with a clear, living root.

04
The product system

One node, many forms

Pull a single node from the helix, warm it, and it becomes the core at the heart of every product. Around that core sits a cool-spectrum form that names the product. The rule runs two ways.

Primitives

Core constant · shell varies

The warm core stays; the cool form changes — an iris, ledger rings, confluent arcs.

Atlas house

Sphere constant · heart varies

Atlas solutions share one cool world; only the warm heart at its centre changes.
